An easier way to try VectorCraft

This site simply makes VectorCraft easier to try without installing it. VectorCraft is developed by the ArtCraft Team and contributors. This mirror is not operated or endorsed by them.

You are loading the official v0.6.0 WebAssembly module with a community bootstrap. The web and desktop apps share an editing engine, but their features are not identical.

  • Open files through the browser picker or drag and drop. Saving and exporting download new files without overwriting originals. Desktop folders and TCP/MCP services are unavailable.
  • The official web app provides recovery in browser storage, subject to quota, private browsing and data clearing. Download saves explicitly; recovery is not a backup.
  • WebGPU or WebGL2 is required. Large vector documents, fonts and heavy operations depend on browser memory. Mobile scaling adjusts display size; it does not provide a complete touch interface.
  • Browser restrictions do not imply the same restrictions on desktop. VectorCraft itself is still alpha; the desktop app is not guaranteed to have every feature complete.

Based on the v0.6.0 source, not an exhaustive test of every editing feature. Keep backups and evaluate the official desktop app before production work.
